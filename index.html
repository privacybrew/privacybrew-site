Android gives you code names. We give you sentences.

Each permission becomes one short sentence about you.

android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
→
It can see where you are, to a few metres.
android.permission.NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES
→
It can find other devices on your Wi-Fi.
com.google.firebase.analytics
→
A tracker from Google. It counts what you do in the app.

Not all access is bad.

The question is simple: does the access match the app?
