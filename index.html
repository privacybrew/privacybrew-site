What PrivacyBrew does

One scan, the whole picture.

Everything below runs the moment you open the app — no setup, no waiting, no account to create.

Your privacy snapshot

Every app scanned at a glance — unusual access, tracker counts, and apps you may have forgotten about, surfaced first so you know exactly where to look.

App-by-app detail

Tap any app to see exactly what it can access, which parts are unusual for what it does, and how it compares to what its developer discloses on the Play Store.

Data-broker opt-out

Direct links to the "people search" sites that sell your info regardless of which apps you use — optional steps, laid out plainly.

Plain-language Learn

No jargon, no scare tactics — short answers to the questions people actually have about permissions and where data goes.

Unusual access, flagged

A messaging app wanting your camera makes sense. A flashlight app wanting your contacts doesn't. We flag the mismatch, not every permission.

Nothing leaves your phone

PrivacyBrew has no INTERNET permission — it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere. Every screen is recomputed live from your device, every time.

How it works

Three steps, no setup.

01 — SCAN

Read what Android already knows

PrivacyBrew asks Android's PackageManager for the apps installed on your device and their already-granted permissions — plain system metadata, not access to what's inside those apps.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Translated, not just listed

Raw permission strings become plain sentences: what an app can access, whether that's typical for what it does, and how many trackers were detected inside it.

03 — ACT

Decide what happens next

Jump straight to the right Android Settings page to revoke access, check an app's Play Store data-safety label, or opt out of a data broker — all optional, all up to you.
