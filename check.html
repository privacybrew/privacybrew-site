Browser Privacy Check

What does a website learn about you?

The moment you open any website, your browser hands it a stack of details about your device, without asking. This page shows you exactly what, and what it means.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing on this page is sent to PrivacyBrew or anyone else, unless you choose to run one of the clearly marked optional tests at the bottom.
Running checks…

1. What every site sees when you arrive

2. Your browser fingerprint

3. Privacy signals your browser sends

4. Third-party cookies

5. Optional tests

Heads up: these two tests can only work by contacting outside companies. They're off until you start them.

Do you have a tracker blocker?

We try to load a few well-known tracking scripts (Google Analytics, Google's ad network, Meta, Microsoft) and see whether something stops them.

Before you run it: if nothing blocks them, your browser really does contact Google, Meta and Microsoft, and they see your IP address. No cookies are sent, and nothing goes to PrivacyBrew.

Can your IP address leak past a VPN?

A browser feature for video calls (WebRTC) can reveal your real IP address even when you use a VPN.

Before you run it: this asks a public Google server to report your IP address back to your browser, so Google sees the request. The result is shown only to you.

Have Android? The PrivacyBrew app does the same for the apps on your phone.
